ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply?

Auto loans are one of the easiest credit products to get approved — available for salaried, self-employed, and business owners with a stable income.

  • ✓Indian resident aged 21–65 years
  • ✓Salaried: minimum monthly income ₹15,000; self-employed: ITR of ₹1.5L+ per year
  • ✓CIBIL score of 650 or above (700+ for best rates)
  • ✓Minimum 1 year of employment / 2 years of business vintage
  • ✓Residence stability — at least 1 year at current address
  • ✓Valid driving licence (helps but not mandatory for all lenders)

Documents Required

  • ✓Aadhaar Card & PAN Card
  • ✓Latest 3 months' salary slips or ITR
  • ✓Bank statements (last 3–6 months)
  • ✓Car dealer quotation / proforma invoice
  • ✓Residence address proof
  • ✓Passport-size photograph

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get an auto loan for a used car?
Yes, used car loans are available for vehicles up to 7 years old. The loan-to-value (LTV) for used cars is typically 70–80% of the valuation. Interest rates for used cars are slightly higher than new car loans (1–3% more) due to higher risk. We partner with lenders who have strong used car loan programs.
What is the maximum funding I can get on an auto loan?
For new cars, most banks fund up to 85–90% of the on-road price. Some lenders offer up to 100% ex-showroom price for high-CIBIL salaried applicants. For used cars, funding is typically 70–80% of the assessed market value. The exact amount depends on your income, CIBIL score, and the lender's policies.
Is there any prepayment charge on auto loans?
Most banks allow foreclosure after 6–12 EMIs with a prepayment charge of 2–5% on the outstanding principal. Some lenders offer zero foreclosure charges after 2 years. We help you select a lender with flexible prepayment terms if early repayment is part of your plan.
Can I get an auto loan with a low CIBIL score?
Auto loans are secured (the car serves as collateral), so some lenders are more flexible on CIBIL compared to personal loans. Applicants with CIBIL 650–700 can often get approved, though at higher interest rates. We identify the right lender based on your profile to maximise approval chances.
Does EMI start before I take delivery of the car?
Typically, your first EMI starts one month after disbursal to the dealer. Since disbursal and delivery usually happen simultaneously, EMIs start from the month after delivery. For pre-delivery disbursal cases, the bank may charge simple interest (pre-EMI) until the first full EMI kicks in.
