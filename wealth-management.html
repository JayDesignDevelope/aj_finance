Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between wealth management and regular investment advisory?
Investment advisory focuses only on which investments to buy. Wealth management is holistic — it covers investments, insurance, loans, tax, and estate planning as an integrated system. A wealth manager looks at how all components interact: your home loan EMI affects how much you can invest in SIPs, which in turn affects your tax planning.
Is wealth management only for the very wealthy?
No. While private banks serve ultra-HNIs (Rs.5 Crore+ portfolio), effective wealth management starts to add significant value from a portfolio of Rs.25 lakhs. At this level, the interaction between tax planning, investments, loans, and insurance is complex enough to benefit from coordinated advice.
How does AAR ZEN CAPITAL make money if advisory is free?
We earn trail commission from mutual fund companies (paid by AMCs, not deducted from your returns), referral fees from insurance companies, and loan processing fees from banking partners. This is disclosed upfront. On the direct plan side for large portfolios, we charge a transparent advisory fee.
Can I continue with my existing investments and advisors?
Yes. We can work alongside your existing CA, stockbroker, and insurance agent — filling in gaps and providing a consolidated view. Many clients consolidate over time as they see the value of having everything coordinated.
What happens to my portfolio if my wealth manager leaves AAR ZEN CAPITAL?
Your investments, insurance policies, and loans are in your name — not tied to any individual. You will be transitioned to a new wealth manager and can review all existing recommendations. Client continuity is protected by our client record management and investment tracking systems.
