ELIGIBILITY

LAP Eligibility Criteria

Available for salaried and self-employed individuals. The property must be free from legal disputes and registered in the applicant's name.

  • ✓Age 21–65 years at time of application (up to loan maturity for some lenders)
  • ✓Salaried: minimum Rs.40,000 per month net income; Self-employed: Rs.5L annual profit
  • ✓CIBIL score of 680 or above (700+ for best rates)
  • ✓Property must be in the applicant's name or co-applicant's name
  • ✓Clear title — no mortgage, attachment, or legal dispute on the property
  • ✓Property in an approved city/location list of the lender

Documents Required

  • ✓Aadhaar and PAN (all applicants and co-applicants)
  • ✓Salary slips / ITR for last 2–3 years
  • ✓Bank statements — 12 months
  • ✓Property title deed / sale deed / agreement to sell
  • ✓Approved building plan and occupancy certificate
  • ✓Latest property tax receipts

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take a loan against a property I am currently renting out?
Yes, rental properties are eligible for LAP. In fact, the monthly rental income can be added to your salary or business income for eligibility calculation, potentially increasing your loan amount. The property must be legally registered in your name and have a clean title.
What happens to my property during the LAP tenure?
Your property remains in your possession and you continue to use it as you like. The lender holds the original title documents as security. Once the loan is fully repaid, all documents are returned to you. There is no restriction on renting out or occupying the property.
Is LAP available for under-construction properties?
Most banks prefer fully constructed, ready-to-occupy, or completed properties with an occupancy certificate for LAP. Some lenders do provide LAP against properties with completion certificates even if not yet registered. Under-construction properties are generally not eligible.
What is the maximum LTV (Loan-to-Value) ratio for LAP?
RBI guidelines cap LAP LTV at 60% for commercial properties and 65–75% for residential properties depending on loan size. In practice, most lenders offer 50–65% to maintain a safety buffer. Location, property type, and your repayment capacity all influence the final LTV offered.
Can I prepay my LAP without penalty?
For floating rate LAP, there is no prepayment penalty as per RBI guidelines applicable to all scheduled commercial banks. Fixed rate LAP may carry a foreclosure charge of 1–3%. NBFCs may have different policies. We disclose all charges upfront before you sign any document.
