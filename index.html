How to improve your CIBIL score

1Pay all EMIs and credit card bills on time — payment history is 35% of your score.
2Keep credit utilisation below 30% of your total credit limit at all times.
3Avoid multiple loan applications in a short period — each creates a hard inquiry.
4Maintain a healthy mix of secured (home/auto) and unsecured (personal) credit.
