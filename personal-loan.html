Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get a personal loan with a CIBIL score below 700?
While most banks prefer a CIBIL score of 700+, some NBFCs and fintech lenders may approve personal loans for scores between 650–700 at higher interest rates. We assess your complete credit profile and match you with the most suitable lender. For scores below 650, we recommend working on credit improvement first — we offer free guidance on this.
What can I use the personal loan amount for?
A personal loan can be used for virtually any legitimate personal expense — wedding arrangements, overseas travel, home renovation, medical bills, higher education fees, consumer durables, or consolidating high-interest credit card debt. Unlike a home or auto loan, there are no end-use restrictions.
How is the interest rate on a personal loan calculated?
Personal loan interest is calculated on a reducing balance basis. As you repay EMIs, the outstanding principal reduces, and interest is charged only on the remaining balance. The effective annual rate (EAR) depends on your CIBIL score, employer category, income level, existing liabilities, and the lender's internal risk assessment.
Is there a prepayment penalty on personal loans?
Most banks allow prepayment after 6–12 EMIs with a foreclosure charge of 2–4% on the outstanding principal. Some lenders offer zero prepayment charges after 12 months. We help you choose a lender with flexible prepayment terms based on your repayment plan.
What happens if I miss an EMI?
Missing an EMI results in a late payment penalty (typically ₹200–₹500 per missed EMI) and negatively impacts your CIBIL score. Consistent non-payment can lead to legal recovery action by the lender. If you anticipate difficulty, contact us or the lender in advance — many banks offer temporary EMI moratoriums for genuine hardship cases.
