ELIGIBILITY

Who Can Apply?

MSME loans are available to registered micro, small, and medium enterprises across manufacturing and service sectors with a valid Udyam registration.

  • ✓Registered MSME with valid Udyam Aadhaar / Udyam Registration Certificate
  • ✓Business vintage of at least 1–2 years in the same activity
  • ✓Annual turnover consistent with the MSME classification limits
  • ✓GST registered entity with regular return filing (preferred)
  • ✓Promoter CIBIL score of 650 or above
  • ✓Business should not be in default with any other lender

Documents Required

  • ✓Udyam Registration Certificate
  • ✓KYC of promoter (Aadhaar & PAN)
  • ✓GST registration certificate & returns
  • ✓ITR with P&L and balance sheet (2 years)
  • ✓Bank account statements (12 months)
  • ✓Business address proof and ownership documents

Frequently Asked Questions

What is CGTMSE and how does it help MSMEs?
CGTMSE (Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises) is a Government of India initiative that provides guarantee cover to banks and NBFCs for MSME loans without collateral. This means you can get a loan up to ₹2 crore without pledging any property or assets — the government backs the lender's risk. The guarantee fee is borne by the lender in most cases.
What is the difference between a micro, small, and medium enterprise?
As per revised MSME definition (2020): Micro enterprise — investment up to ₹1 crore and turnover up to ₹5 crore. Small enterprise — investment up to ₹10 crore and turnover up to ₹50 crore. Medium enterprise — investment up to ₹50 crore and turnover up to ₹250 crore. Both manufacturing and service sector enterprises are included.
Is Udyam registration mandatory for an MSME loan?
Udyam registration is mandatory for availing government-backed schemes like CGTMSE, MUDRA, and interest subvention benefits. For regular bank MSME loans, some lenders may process without Udyam, but registration is highly recommended as it also unlocks various government subsidies, tender preferences, and priority in government procurement.
Can a new MSME (less than 1 year old) get a loan?
While most banks prefer 1–2 years of business vintage, MUDRA Shishu (up to ₹50,000) and MUDRA Kishore (up to ₹5 lakhs) loans are available for newer businesses through microfinance institutions and banks. Some NBFCs also offer startup MSME loans based on business plan and promoter's personal creditworthiness.
What is the PSB59minute portal for MSME loans?
PSB59minute (psbloansin59minutes.com) is a government portal that provides in-principle MSME loan approval within 59 minutes. It is available for loans from ₹1 lakh to ₹5 crore. After in-principle approval, the actual disbursement is done by the selected partner bank within 7–15 days. We assist MSMEs in applying on this portal and navigating the subsequent bank process.
