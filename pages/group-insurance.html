GMC vs GPA — What's the Difference?

Group Mediclaim (GMC)

Health insurance for hospitalisation expenses. Covers employees and optionally their families.

  • ✓ Hospitalisation & surgeries
  • ✓ Pre & post hospitalisation
  • ✓ Maternity benefit (optional)
  • ✓ OPD cover (optional)
  • ✓ No medical check-up required
  • ✓ Cashless at 10,000+ hospitals

Group Personal Accident (GPA)

Accidental death and disability cover. Pays lump sum compensation for accidents at work or elsewhere.

  • ✓ Accidental death (100% SI)
  • ✓ Permanent total disability (100%)
  • ✓ Permanent partial disability
  • ✓ Temporary total disability
  • ✓ Workmen's Compensation compliance
  • ✓ 24×7 worldwide cover
