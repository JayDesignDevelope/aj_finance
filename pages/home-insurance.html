WHY HOME INSURANCE

Why Every Homeowner Needs It

Protect Your Largest Asset

Your home is often worth crores. A single fire or flood can cause damage worth years of savings — covered under one policy.

Extremely Affordable

Home insurance is one of the most underutilised yet affordable covers — starting at just ₹1,000 per year for a ₹50 lakh home.

Often Required by Lenders

Many banks require home insurance as a condition of the home loan. We help you choose the right policy that satisfies lender requirements.

Reinstatement Value

Good policies pay reconstruction cost (not depreciated value) — so you can actually rebuild your home after a total loss.

Alternate Accommodation

Some plans cover hotel/rent costs while your home is being repaired after an insured event — a hugely underrated benefit.

Quick Claim Settlement

Home insurance claims are typically settled within 7–15 working days with proper documentation. We assist with the entire claim process.

Common Questions

Home insurance is not legally mandatory in India, but it is strongly recommended and often required by banks when you take a home loan. Banks may require fire and allied perils cover at a minimum for the loan tenure. Given the low cost and high coverage, it is one of the most cost-effective financial protections available.

Land cannot be damaged by fire or flood, so insuring it is unnecessary. For a flat, this means the construction cost per sq ft × your carpet area, not the circle rate value.
