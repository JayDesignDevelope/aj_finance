Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start a mutual fund SIP with just ₹500?
Yes. Many top-performing equity funds, including large-cap and ELSS funds, accept SIPs starting at ₹500 per month. You can start small and increase your investment as your income grows — a feature called SIP top-up.
How are mutual fund returns taxed?
For equity funds, gains from redemptions held over 1 year are taxed at 10% as Long Term Capital Gains (LTCG) above ₹1 lakh per year. Short-term gains (under 1 year) are taxed at 15%. Debt fund gains are taxed at your income tax slab rate regardless of holding period.
What is the difference between regular and direct mutual funds?
Direct plans have lower expense ratios because there's no distributor commission. Over 10–15 years, the difference in returns can be significant — often 0.5–1% per annum. Our advisors recommend direct plans where they deliver better long-term value after accounting for advisory costs.
Can I withdraw my mutual fund investment anytime?
Most open-ended mutual funds allow redemption anytime, with proceeds credited to your bank account within 1–3 business days. ELSS funds have a mandatory 3-year lock-in. Some funds charge an exit load (typically 1%) if you redeem within 1 year of investment.
Are mutual funds safe? Can I lose all my money?
Mutual funds are market-linked and carry varying degrees of risk. Equity funds can see short-term losses but historically deliver inflation-beating returns over 5+ years. Debt funds carry lower risk. Diversified portfolios and long investment horizons significantly reduce the risk of permanent capital loss.
