WHY ULIP

Life insurance protection combined with equity/debt investment — your family stays covered while your wealth grows.

Tax Deduction Under 80C

Premiums up to ₹1.5 lakh per year qualify for deduction under Section 80C, reducing your taxable income.

Tax-Free Maturity (10(10D))

Maturity proceeds are completely tax-free under Section 10(10D), subject to conditions — a huge advantage over MF returns.

Fund Switching 4x/Year Free

Partial Withdrawal After 5 Years

Transparent Fund Options

Choose from equity, balanced, bond, or liquid funds based on your risk appetite — with full visibility into NAV and charges.

Term + MF gives more flexibility and potentially higher returns if you're disciplined — but ULIP's tax-free exit (for premiums up to 2.5L/year) is a strong advantage for high earners. ULIPs have a mandatory 5-year lock-in period. You cannot surrender or partially withdraw before 5 years. After 5 years, partial withdrawals are allowed tax-free. Most financial advisors recommend staying invested for at least 10 years to maximise returns and offset initial charges.

ULIPs offer dual tax benefits: (1) Premiums up to ₹1.5 lakh per year qualify for deduction under Section 80C. (2) Maturity proceeds are tax-free under Section 10(10D), provided the annual premium does not exceed ₹2.5 lakh for policies issued after Feb 2021. This makes ULIPs one of the most tax-efficient investment products in India.

ULIPs have several charges: Premium Allocation Charge (upfront, typically 1–5% in early years), Fund Management Charge (0.5–1.35% per year on fund value), Mortality Charge (for life cover, increases with age), Policy Administration Charge, and Switching Charges (4 free switches per year, then nominal fee). IRDAI has capped total charges, making newer ULIPs far more competitive than older ones.

Additional switches may attract a nominal charge (typically ₹100–250 per switch). This feature allows you to dynamically manage your asset allocation without tax implications.
